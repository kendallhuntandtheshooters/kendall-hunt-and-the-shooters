The Band

Kendall Hunt and The Shooters are a five-piece Texas country and red dirt band from Forney, Texas — big vocals, driving guitars, and a setlist built for a good time.

From Texas country and red dirt to 80s, 90s, and 2000s rock, the band plays bars, rodeos, city events, private parties, and festivals. They've opened for Aaron Watson, Josh Abbott, and Mike Ryan, packed the American Airlines Center Plaza for the Dallas Stars, and played rooms like Hank's, Filthy McNasty's Saloon, Red River Station, Union 28, Jack's Tavern, and The Gar Hole.

  • Kendall Hunt — lead vocals & guitar
  • Zach — lead guitar
  • Collin — keys
  • Corey — bass
  • Chris — drums
