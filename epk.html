Bio

Kendall Hunt and The Shooters started the way a lot of Texas bands do, at open mic nights, EJ's in McKinney and The Gar Hole in Westminster, playing for whoever was in the room. Six years later the rooms have grown, but the idea has not changed. It does not matter where you play. What matters is a crowd all wanting the same thing, to forget real life for a couple of hours and party like there's no tomorrow.

Along the way the band has opened for Aaron Watson, Josh Abbott, Mike Ryan, Hayden Haddock, Cody Hibbard, John Baumann, Tanner Fenoglio, and Ricochet, and played rooms from Hank's and Red River Station to the American Airlines Center plaza and the White Deer Rodeo. The band has six singles out on Spotify and Apple Music, including "The Country Man Anthem" and "Too Damn Good," with more songs headed to the studio.

We're just getting started.
